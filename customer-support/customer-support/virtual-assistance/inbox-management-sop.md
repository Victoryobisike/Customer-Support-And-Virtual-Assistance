# Standard Operating Procedure (SOP): Executive Inbox & Calendar Management

**Purpose:** Maintain an organized, zero-backlog inbox and conflict-free schedule so leadership stays focused on high-priority work.  
**Tools:** Gmail / Outlook, Google Calendar, Slack / Microsoft Teams

---

## 1. Daily Inbox Triage Schedule
* **Morning Sweep (8:30 AM – 9:00 AM):** Process overnight emails, flag urgent client/partner messages, and confirm the day's meeting links.
* **Midday Check (1:00 PM – 1:15 PM):** Clear new enquiries, send scheduled follow-ups, and update calendar changes.
* **End-of-Day Wrap (4:30 PM – 4:45 PM):** Ensure all urgent threads have responses, move pending items to *Awaiting Reply*, and send a 3-bullet daily summary to the executive.

---

## 2. Folder & Label System
| Label | Color | Criteria | Target Response Time |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1 - Urgent / Action Needed** | Red | Client escalations, same-day deadlines, executive approvals | Within 1 hour |
| **2 - Follow-Up / In Progress** | Orange | Ongoing coordination, scheduling requests, vendor quotes | Within 4 hours |
| **3 - Awaiting Reply** | Blue | Sent messages waiting on external confirmation (reviewed every 48 hrs) | 48-hour nudge |
| **4 - Receipts & Admin** | Gray | Invoices, software subscriptions, newsletters, automated confirmations | Weekly archive |

---

## 3. Calendar Management Rules
* **Buffer Times:** Always schedule a **15-minute buffer** between back-to-back external calls.
* **Focus Blocks:** Protect **9:00 AM – 11:00 AM** daily as *Deep Work / No Meetings* unless explicitly approved.
* **Timezone Verification:** Double-check participant time zones before sending invites and always include a Zoom/Teams link and 2-bullet agenda in the calendar description.
