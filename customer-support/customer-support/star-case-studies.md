# Customer Support Case Studies (STAR Method)

## Case Study 1: Turning Repeated Customer Enquiries into Self-Service Clarity
* **Situation:** Customers frequently contacted support with the same questions regarding onboarding steps, account setup, and basic troubleshooting, creating avoidable queue backlogs.
* **Task:** Reduce repetitive enquiry volume while maintaining fast, empathetic support for complex issues.
* **Action:** Categorized incoming customer questions over a two-week period to identify the top five recurring confusion points. Drafted clear, plain-language response templates and a step-by-step quick-start guide for customers.
* **Result:** Cut average response time on routine enquiries, improved first-contact resolution consistency, and freed up capacity to handle urgent escalations calmly.

## Case Study 2: Resolving a High-Friction Escalation Through Structured Troubleshooting
* **Situation:** A frustrated user reported repeated service interruptions and felt their previous messages had not addressed the root cause.
* **Task:** De-escalate the conversation, isolate the technical bottleneck, and restore trust.
* **Action:** Acknowledged the customer's frustration without defensiveness, summarized their issue to confirm understanding, and walked through a structured diagnostic checklist (drawing on my engineering troubleshooting background) to pinpoint the exact configuration error rather than offering a temporary workaround.
* **Result:** Resolved the underlying issue on the first structured follow-up, turning a dissatisfied user into a positive promoter.
