# Customer De-Escalation & Response Templates

These sample macros demonstrate my approach to customer communication: **acknowledge the customer's frustration first, take ownership, explain the next step clearly, and close warmly.**

---

## Scenario 1: Delayed Delivery / Missed Timeline
**Context:** A customer reaches out frustrated because their order has not arrived on the promised date.

> **Subject:** Update on your order #[Order_Number] – We’re on it
>
> Hi [Customer Name],
>
> Thank you for reaching out, and I completely understand how frustrating it is to still be waiting on your order—especially when you planned around today's delivery window.
>
> I have checked directly with our dispatch team and confirmed that your package is currently in transit at the [Hub Name] facility due to a routing delay. I have flagged your order for priority delivery, and it is scheduled to arrive tomorrow, **[Day, Date], between [Time Window]**.
>
> You can track live updates here: [Tracking Link]. I have also set a personal reminder to check on your delivery tomorrow afternoon to make sure it reaches you safely.
>
> Please reply directly to this message if you need any adjustments to your delivery instructions.
>
> Warm regards,  
> **Victory Obisike**  
> Customer Support Specialist

## Scenario 2: Billing Error / Duplicate Charge
**Context:** A customer notices they were charged twice and is worried about losing money.

> **Subject:** Resolved: Duplicate charge on your account (#[Ticket_ID])
>
> Hi [Customer Name],
>
> Thank you for flagging this right away—I completely understand why seeing two charges would be concerning, and I apologize for the inconvenience.
>
> I reviewed your billing statement and confirmed that the second charge of **[Amount]** was a duplicate authorization error. I have already processed a full refund of **[Amount]** back to your original payment method (Ref: `#[Refund_ID]`).
>
> **What to expect next:**
> * Your refund confirmation receipt is attached to this email.
> * Depending on your bank, the funds will appear in your statement within **2–3 business days**.
>
> Your subscription and account access remain completely uninterrupted. Please let me know if you have any questions at all!
>
> Best regards,  
> **Victory Obisike**  
> Customer Support Specialist

## Scenario 3: Saying "No" to an Out-of-Policy Refund Request
**Context:** A customer requests a full cash refund outside the 14-day return window, but we want to retain their goodwill.

> **Subject:** Your return request for order #[Order_Number]
>
> Hi [Customer Name],
>
> Thank you for sharing your feedback with us. I understand that the item wasn't quite what you expected, and I’d love to help find the best solution for you.
>
> Because your order was delivered on **[Date]**, it falls outside our standard 14-day window for a full card refund. However, because we value having you as a customer, I can offer two alternatives today:
>
> 1. **Instant Store Credit:** A 100% credit of **[Amount]** issued immediately to your account with no expiration date.
> 2. **Free Product Exchange:** We can swap the item for another size or model at zero shipping cost.
>
> Let me know which option works best for you, and I will set it up right away!
>
> Warm regards,  
> **Victory Obisike**  
> Customer Support Specialist
